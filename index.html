About the Platform

About AuditQMS Pro Suite

AuditQMS Pro Suite Enterprise Edition 2.0 is an integrated SHEQ Management Platform designed to help organizations simplify quality, safety, health, environmental, and compliance management.

The platform helps organizations move away from paper-based processes by connecting audits, findings, corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), risk management, document control, performance monitoring, and management reviews in one digital solution.

Built with an offline-first approach, AuditQMS Pro Suite enables users to continue capturing important quality records even in environments with limited connectivity. Audit records, findings, CAPA actions, risks, documents, client workspaces, and company settings can be securely stored locally on the device using browser storage technology.

Designed for industries including food manufacturing, laboratories, pharmaceuticals, warehousing, and other compliance-driven organizations, AuditQMS Pro Suite provides a smarter way to manage continuous improvement and operational excellence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. AuditQMS Pro Suite runs in your browser as a progressive web app — you can also add it to your home screen on mobile for an app-like experience, with no app store required.

Does it work without internet access?

Yes. The platform is offline-first — audits, findings, CAPAs, and risk records are captured locally on your device and sync automatically when you're back online.

Which standards does it support?

AuditQMS Pro Suite ships with checklist templates aligned to ISO 9001, ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, ISO 45001, BRCGS, and HACCP, plus South African references including R638 of 2018 and the OHS Act 85 of 1993. Custom checklists are also supported.

Can multiple auditors work on the same audit?

Yes. Every audit supports a Lead Auditor plus a full audit team, with clause references, finding numbers, and photo evidence on every checklist item and finding.

Is my data secure?

Audit records, findings, CAPA actions, risks, documents, and company settings are stored locally on your device using browser storage technology, with optional cloud sync. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
