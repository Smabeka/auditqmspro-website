FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does AuditQMS work offline?

Yes. The platform is offline-first for field audits and checklists. Data syncs when connectivity returns.

Can we export PDF reports?

Yes. Audit and CAPA records can be exported as PDF reports suitable for external audits and management review.

Do you support digital signatures?

Yes. Digital signatures are supported on relevant records so sign-off is tracked and auditable.

Which standards does it support?

Teams use AuditQMS against ISO 9001, ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, ISO 45001, BRCGS, HACCP, R638 of 2018 and OHS Act 85 of 1993 — mapped to your own checklists and documents.
